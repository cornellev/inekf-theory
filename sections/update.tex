
\subsection{Update}
Our filter propogates variance using right-invariant error $\eta = \hat \chi \chi^T$
Our pseudo measurements are right-invariant adn can be applied directly to the errror.The GNSS measurements however are left invariant and have constant (or close to constant for biases) jacobians only in left-invariant coordinates. \\
To mitigate this, we transform the covariance amtrix to left-invariant coordinates for the update. The cost will be 4 matrix multiplies.\\
Let us first define our adjoint $Ad_\mathcal{X}$ which is defined as the unique amtrix which satisfies $$\mathcal{X} \exp(\delta^\wedge)\mathcal{X}^{-1} = \exp((Ad_\mathcal{X})^\wedge)$$\\
The relation of right and left ivnariant errors is defined as:
$$\xi^R = \mathrm{Ad}_{\hat\chi}\,\xi^L, \qquad \mathrm{Ad}_{\hat\chi}^{-1} = \mathrm{Ad}_{\hat\chi^{-1}}$$\\

0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & \hat R & 0 \\

\end{bmatrix}$$,\\$$ \qquad Q_c = \mathrm{diag}\big(\Sigma_g,\ \Sigma_a,\ 0,\ \Sigma_{bg},\ \Sigma_{ba},\ \Sigma_l\big)
